(02) — Case Study

Zaytouna Park.

A synchronized hospitality POS connecting table orders, cashier terminals, and kitchen displays — every device sharing the same live view of the venue, with sub-second state synchronization.

Role — Hospitality POS Stack — Flutter · Supabase Sector — Restaurants & venues
Sub-second
Order state shared across every terminal and kitchen screen
Table → Kitchen
Orders route straight from the floor to the kitchen display
Flutter
One codebase across cashier, waiter, and kitchen devices
The Challenge

A busy venue runs on timing. Orders taken at the table, rung up at the cashier, and cooked in the kitchen had to stay in step — without paper tickets or shouted relays.

The Approach

We built a Flutter POS on a Supabase realtime backend so every surface subscribes to the same order state. A change at one terminal appears everywhere — the cashier, the floor, and the kitchen display.

The Outcome

Staff work from one shared, always-current view of the venue: tables, open orders, and kitchen progress stay in sync from the first order to the final bill.
