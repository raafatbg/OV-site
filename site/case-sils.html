(03) — Case Study

Sils.

A cloud-native POS platform delivering isolated tenant environments, live analytics, and Arabic-first workflows — one product serving many independent retail businesses across Lebanon.

Role — Platform Engineering Stack — Flutter · Supabase Sector — Retail SaaS
Isolated
Each business runs in its own tenant, with its own data and configuration
Live
Sales and inventory analytics update as transactions land
Arabic-first
Right-to-left workflows and bilingual receipts from day one
The Challenge

Independent retailers needed a modern POS without a custom build each — but every business still needs its own data, settings, and branding kept strictly apart.

The Approach

We engineered Sils as a multi-tenant platform: a shared Flutter product on Supabase, with tenant isolation enforced at the data layer so each business only ever sees its own records.

The Outcome

New retailers can be onboarded onto a ready platform, with live analytics and an Arabic-native interface their staff are fluent in from the first shift.
