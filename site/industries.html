(04) — Industries

Built for how you trade

Every sector runs on a different rhythm. We design around yours — from a spare-parts counter at rush hour to a restaurant floor on a Saturday night.

01 — Retail & spare parts

Heavy catalogues, fast counters

Parts stores, hardware and specialist retail live and die by how fast the counter moves. We build tills that find any item in under a second and keep selling when the power goes.

The challenge

  • Thousands of SKUs and part numbers
  • Queues at the counter during rush hour
  • Power and network drops mid-sale

What we build

  • Sub-second parts and barcode lookup
  • Offline-first tills with local integrity
  • Dual-currency pricing and reconciliation

02 — Restaurants, cafés & venues

Floor, till and kitchen in sync

A busy service depends on timing. We connect tables, cashiers and kitchen screens so every order lands in the right place, the moment it is taken.

The challenge

  • Orders lost between table and kitchen
  • WhatsApp and delivery orders off the books
  • Peak-hour pressure on a small team

What we build

  • Table ordering and cashier terminals
  • Real-time kitchen display routing
  • Every channel captured in one system

03 — Multi-branch & chains

Every branch, one picture

Growing past one location multiplies the blind spots. We give owners one live view of stock, cash and performance across every branch.

The challenge

  • Stock and cash scattered across locations
  • Pricing that drifts between branches
  • Reports that arrive weeks too late

What we build

  • Central back-office with per-branch control
  • Live dashboards across every location
  • Branch-level permissions and audit trails
